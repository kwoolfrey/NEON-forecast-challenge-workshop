Part 1:

Honestly, it’s kinda hard to assess which forecast did better at matching the observations when they’re all plotted on the same graph. But I think the climatology forecast slightly outperformed the other two. None of the forecasts were able to capture the jump in temperature at the beginning of April. While the persistence forecast had the most uncertainty, I wouldn’t say that helped it in capturing the observations very well. It seems like it’s high uncertainty didn’t really pay off.

This graph also isn’t the prettiest and I’m sorry that the color schemes don’t match. From this, it appears that climatology has the lowest CRPS throughout the study area, other than a period in mid-April where the Zimmerman forecast outperformed it. I would say the two performed quite similarly. It’s clear in this plot that the persistence model performed the poorest.
